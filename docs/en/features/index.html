PasteFerry features

Copy and paste,
the way it should be.

One clipboard for your Mac, Windows and Linux computers.

Download PasteFerry ▶ Watch the full tour

Copy here.
Paste there.

⌘CCtrl+C here, ⌘VCtrl+V there. No shared account needed.

MacBook⌘C
WindowsCtrl+V
LinuxCtrl+V

  • Text, images, files, folders

    Pastes cleanly into plain-text fields.

  • Up to 50 MB is sent as you copy

    Larger items on demand. The limit is adjustable.

  • Pause any time

    Pause everything, or just one computer.

Any size.
Just drag it over.

Computers near and far are on the radar. Drag files onto one to send them. No cloud drive, and your history stays clean.

  • Any size

    Multi-GB videos or folders with thousands of files.

  • Picks up where it stopped

    Resumes after a dropped connection or sleep. Every file is verified.

  • Received files in one place

    Saved to Downloads by default, one click to show.

Works across networks; relaying through Service is limited to 100 MB a day. Both computers need 2.5 or later.

New in 2.6

A friend’s computer,
just as easy.

Add them as a contact, no pairing needed. Files arrive once they accept, with an optional note.

  • They decide what comes in

    See who sent what before accepting, or auto-accept people you trust.

  • You decide who can send

    No one, contacts only, or everyone on your network for 10 minutes.

  • Files, not chat

    Contacts never see your clipboard, history or devices.

On different networks, both turn on Service. Both need 2.6 or later.

Home and office,
connected directly.

Same network: install and pair. Different networks: turn on Service.

  • Same network

    No server. Enter a pairing code and you’re done.

  • Across networks

    Service, your own server, or Tailscale.

  • End-to-end encrypted

    Verify fingerprints when pairing; the server never sees your content.
