Your clipboard
history, at hand.

Press ⌥VAlt+V to search your clipboard history and paste into the current app. Save favorites for later, with a separate copy of each file.

More about quick paste →

Drag it over.
It’s there.

Drag files and whole folders between computers. Large transfers can resume after an interruption, and received files stay together in Downloads.

More about Send Files →

Your phone joins in.

On the same network, Android can send and receive batches of photos and files, then save or share them. On iPhone and iPad, scan a QR code for clipboard history and transfers in the browser; keep the page open.

How to use the phone →

More than a shared clipboard.

  • Send files to contacts

    Add a contact and send files for them to accept. Contacts cannot see your clipboard or history.

  • Nine themes, plus your own

    Default, Pixel and seven more styles have light and dark palettes. Customize colors and backgrounds, or create and apply a theme with AI.

  • Your content stays private

    End-to-end encryption, with fingerprints checked during pairing. Servers help connect devices and relay data, but cannot decrypt your content.
