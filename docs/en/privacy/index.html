Privacy

In short

What you copy, the files you send, their names and your notes travel end-to-end encrypted between your own devices. We can't see them and don't store them.

Service (when turned on)

New installs use the local network only. When you turn on Service, your devices connect to the official server so they can find each other across networks. We keep a pseudonymous device number, first and last connection times and daily relay usage. Only when a direct connection fails and relay is needed does the app derive a PasteFerry-specific machine digest, so that one computer shares one relay allowance. Real device names, raw system identifiers and content are never uploaded.

Update checks

The app checks for updates once a day, fetching a signed update list from the PasteFerry website, GitHub and Gitee. The request to the website includes the version, system and language, and whether it is the first check of the day, week or month, to provide the right update and plan future releases. No device ID or account is included and IP addresses are not stored. Automatic checks can be turned off in Settings → About. Updates install only after you confirm, and only if they match a list signed with the PasteFerry release key.

Contacts

A contact sees the display name and avatar you set, the name and system of the computer you send from, and whether you use the local network, Service or your own server (for your own server, only a digest of its address). Your clipboard, history and other devices are never shared with contacts, and their files arrive only after you accept them.

Website

This website uses Cloudflare Web Analytics and keeps its own totals of page views and download clicks (date, page path, page language, version and file downloaded). No analytics cookies or persistent visitor identifiers are used, and IP addresses and browser identifiers are not stored. With Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control turned on, the website's own counters are not sent.

Discussion and feedback

Nicknames and text you submit are stored on the server for review and support, and become public after approval. When AI review is enabled, necessary text is sent to a third-party AI service such as DeepSeek under that provider's data handling terms; administrators can also read and handle submissions. Your browser saves drafts, submission records and per-message access credentials for up to 30 days. Clearing local data does not withdraw server messages; use the original browser to withdraw them. Unpublished, withdrawn and hidden content is removed after 90 days; public content remains until withdrawn or deleted. Abuse prevention uses temporary source digests rather than storing raw IP addresses. Do not submit passwords, API keys or private information.

How it is used

This information is used to provide and improve the service, publish updates, keep the service secure and prevent abuse, measure and plan, and meet legal obligations. We don't sell it or use it for advertising.

Third parties

GitHub, Gitee, Cloudflare and any server you choose handle your requests to them under their own privacy policies.

Retention

Daily per-device detail for Service is kept for 90 days; daily totals without personal identity are kept indefinitely.

Your choices

You can use the local network only, turn off Service, turn off automatic update checks, or run your own server. The PasteFerry client and server are open source.

Disclaimer and changes

PasteFerry and Service are provided free and as is; relay allowances and features may change or be suspended. We are not liable for data loss or failed syncs caused by networks, devices, third-party services or self-hosted servers. Changes to this notice take effect when published on the website; continuing to use PasteFerry means you accept them.

Contact

Questions about this notice: open an issue at github.com/baiyuze/pasteferry/issues.
